Overview

A full-length program that treats the syllabus as a schedule, not a suggestion. Every chapter has a teaching week, a practice set, a test slot and a review conversation attached to it before the year begins.

Chapter-mapped calendar
The full syllabus laid out week by week, published on day one and visible in the portal all year.
Weekly practice sets
Graded difficulty tiers so a student can start where they are and climb, instead of drowning.
Batch size capped
Sixty seats per classroom batch. The cap is enforced by the admissions system, not by intention.
Rank-linked feedback
Every test returns score, rank, accuracy and time-per-question — not just a number out of 720.
Two revision cycles
A full second pass over the syllabus in the final four months, weighted toward weak chapters.
Parent visibility
Attendance, marks and fee status mirrored to a parent login, updated the same day.
