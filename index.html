The method

Ambition is common. Direction is rare. We teach the second one.

Every Pragati student gets a syllabus map, a weekly test rhythm, a named mentor and a performance record their family can actually read. Nothing about the plan is a secret, and nothing about the progress is a guess.

How we work

A map, not a mood

Chapter-level plans with dates attached. Students always know what week they are in and what comes next.

Testing as feedback

42 assessments a year, each one followed by a review that names the exact chapters costing marks.

One mentor, all year

The same faculty mentor from admission to result day — fortnightly one-to-one reviews, no rotation.

Parents in the loop

Attendance, test scores and fee status visible to families in the portal, updated the same day.

Programs

Six programs.One standard.

Classroom, online and hybrid tracks — each with a fixed batch date, a published fee and a seat count that is the live number, not a marketing number.

Why it moves

Ranks improve when feedback arrives faster than the next test.

Mock results are published within 24 hours, mentors review them inside 48, and the next week's plan is rewritten against the weak chapters that showed up. The loop is short enough that a student can still act on it.

About

Built in Lucknow,for Uttar Pradesh.

Pragati Academy started in 2016 with one classroom in Gomti Nagar and a stubborn idea: that a coaching institute should be judged on how clearly it can explain a student's progress, not on how loudly it can advertise a rank.
